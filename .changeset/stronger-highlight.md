Make a highlighted crew easier to follow. On `BumpsChartMultiYear`, its line from one year to the next is now highlighted too. While a crew is highlighted, the chart's other lines can fade: set `--react-bumps-chart-unselected-opacity` (default `1`, no fade). `--react-bumps-chart-highlight-stroke-width` sets the width of highlighted and blades lines (default `2`).
