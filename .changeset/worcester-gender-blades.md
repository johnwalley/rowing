`Blade` takes an optional `gender` prop (`"men"` or `"women"`) for clubs whose men's and women's blades differ. Worcester College, Oxford (`wro`) is the first: its men's blade is black with a pink cross, and its women's blade, the default, stays pink with a black cross. `BumpsChart` passes its event's gender, so men's charts show the men's blade.
